TL;DR: Project tracking compares actual progress against your plan to catch delays early. Choose pre-built options like Monday.com (visual boards), Asana (task dependencies), Jira (Agile development), or Smartsheet (spreadsheet-style), or build a custom tracker with Bubble’s AI generation and visual editing. Essential features include plan vs. actual tracking, milestone management, visual workflows, automation, and mobile access. The best choice depends on your team’s working style rather than feature count.Most project delays don’t announce themselves. Instead, they accumulate quietly.
